Muscle (muscularis) A type of soft tissue. Perimuscular fibrous tissue A type of connective tissue that surrounds muscle. Serosa An outer membrane that covers the gallbladder and bile ducts. The serosa is also called the serous membrane or visceral peritoneum. Bile duct cancer stages A bile duct is made up of several layers. The inner and outer layers are membranes. A membrane is a very thin layer that covers a surface. In between these membranes are muscle and connective tissue.
